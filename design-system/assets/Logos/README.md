# Logos

The three logo files from the portfolio's `public/logos/` folder, copied unchanged (WebP).

- `essamLogo.webp` — the round mark. Favicons, avatars, small headers.
- `essamLogoBorder.webp` — the mark with its ring. The hero's textured background at about 12% opacity, rotated and scaled; do not use it as a foreground logo.
- `essamLogoWithText.webp` — the mark with the wordmark. Headers and the footer.

Never redraw or recolour a mark. Keep at least the mark's own clear space.
