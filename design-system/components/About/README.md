Intro panel with a large lead line, two paragraphs and a checklist of highlights.

## When to use

- The "About" section of a portfolio or a company page.

## Consumer provides

- `lead` and `emphasis`: the big line. The emphasis part shows in primary.
- `paragraphs`: short paragraphs, `gray-300`.
- `highlightsTitle` and `highlights`: a checklist, two columns from 640px.

## Specs

- Panel `glass-fill` with a `glass-edge` border, `radius-2xl`, padding `space-8` (48px from 768px).
- Lead 24px semibold. Highlight boxes use `border-subtle` and `border-faint`, text `gray-200`.
