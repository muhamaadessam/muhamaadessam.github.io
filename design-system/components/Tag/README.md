Small pill that labels a skill, technology or keyword.

## When to use

- Listing the technologies behind a project, or the skills under the hero.
- Not for actions or navigation; a tag is not clickable in the source.

## Consumer provides

- The label, short and in its own casing (`Flutter`, `Clean Architecture`).

## Specs

- Radius `radius-full`; padding `space-1` vertical, `space-3` horizontal.
- Fill `border-faint`, 1px `border-subtle` edge; label `caption` in `gray-300`.
- Wrap with `space-2` gaps.
